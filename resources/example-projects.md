# Example Projects per Track

Source: [Resources tab, AI x Epistemics Research Sprint](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/ai-x-epistemics-research-sprint-2026-11-13-to-2026-11-15#resources)

These are illustrative project ideas listed on the page, each tagged with the reading-list items it links to. Descriptions are paraphrased; linked anchor text/URLs are reproduced as given.

## Track 1: Model Epistemics & Decision-Making Evals

- **MisleadBench** — multi-turn honesty benchmark scoring the gap between what a model implies and what it will defend under pushback.
  Links: "MASK" https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03750 · "DarkBench" https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10728
- **Confidence Audit** — elicits a model's confidence in its own recommendations and checks it against actual outcomes.
  Links: "ForecastBench" https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19839 · "consistency checks" https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18544
- **FlipRate** — measures how much a model's answer and stated confidence change under escalating rebuttals.
  Links: "SycEval" https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08177 · "ELEPHANT" https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995
- **Epistemic virtue eval suite** — evals built as Inspect tasks.
  Links: "collective-epistemics sketches" https://www.forethought.org/research/design-sketches-collective-epistemics · "Inspect" https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_ai
- **Article 5 measurement protocol** — maps DarkBench/MASK-style measurements onto EU AI Act Article 5(1)(a).
  Links: "EU AI Act" https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/ · "DarkBench" https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10728 · "MASK" https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03750

## Track 2: Trust Infrastructure

- **Track-Record Ledger** — public API for dated claims and source reliability scores.
  Link: "Community Notes" https://github.com/twitter/communitynotes
- **Tamper-Evident Action Recorder** — signed agent logs built on OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions.
  Links: "OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions" https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/registry/attributes/gen-ai/ · "Certificate Transparency" https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962
- **Citation Binder** — verifies whether cited sources actually support the claims attributed to them.
  Link: "AVeriTeC" https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13117
- **Provenance Study** — controlled experiment comparing behavior with and without provenance signals.
  Links: "C2PA" https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/index.html · "Community Notes" https://github.com/twitter/communitynotes

## Track 3: Epistemic Products

- **Dispute Escalation Benchmark** — scores whether chatbots steer mid-dispute conversations toward repair or escalation.
  Link: "evidence this is a live harm" https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aec8352
- **Decision-Brief Rubric** — grading harness for chatbot-generated decision briefings.
  Link: "briefings" https://www.forethought.org/research/design-sketches-angels-on-the-shoulder
- **Crux Mapper** — maps disagreement structure and cruxes in group forecasts/discussions.
  Link: "the Habermas Machine" https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adq2852
