# Swift Centre

## Summary

Swift Centre is a London-based decision-science and foresight organization operating through two linked entities: a Research wing and an Advisory wing. The Research wing produces independent, scenario-based foresight on AI, power concentration, and great-power conflict, including "situation monitors" that combine horizon scanning, scenario analysis, causal modelling, and forecasting. The Advisory wing sells bespoke forecasting consultancy, prediction-market analysis, and workshop-based capability building to clients, drawing on what the site describes as "the top 1% of forecasters in the world, measured, ranked and publicly validated."

## Key Claims / Findings

- The homepage frames the organization's dual structure as applying "the same rigour, pointed two ways" across Research and Advisory.
- Research wing output includes scenario-based research papers and "situation monitors," described as living analyses combining horizon scanning, scenario analysis, causal modelling, and forecasting. Research focus areas named: AI, power concentration, and great-power conflict.
- Advisory wing services: (1) identifying mispriced events on prediction-market platforms; (2) bespoke forecasting consultancy, where Swift identifies a client's key risks and runs a managed scenario-analysis and forecasting process; (3) interactive workshops teaching forecasting methodology.
- Three named client engagements appear on the page:
  - **Google DeepMind** — Swift forecast how future AI systems might perform on DeepMind capability evaluations and whether those capabilities would raise AI's profile as a global issue; the forecasts were incorporated into a published paper (cited on the page as arXiv 2403.13793).
  - **Metrea Aerospace** — Swift forecasters joined the client's internal experts in a temporary combined team, ran online and in-person workshops, and produced a risk report on a volatile region while also transferring forecasting skills to the client's staff.
  - **Open Nuclear Network** (via One Earth Future Austria) — Swift facilitated a conference for technical experts on geopolitical risk, covering forecasting principles, calibration exercises, and elicitation methods; the resulting estimates reportedly informed the client's risk response over several years.
- Two testimonials are quoted, from Sarah Laderman (Open Nuclear Network / One Earth Future Austria) and Christian Holland (Metrea), both describing the engagements favorably, with Holland noting improved calibration among his own team.
- The page links to a public forecasts archive and a Substack, plus a contact form and the address info@swiftcentre.org.
- No individual team members, dates of founding, or methodology white papers were named or visible on this page.
